# SlotBack — Project Overview

## Problem
Small service businesses lose appointment capacity to cancellations while other customers may be waiting. Manual outreach and coordination take staff time and make recovery outcomes difficult to measure.

## Solution
SlotBack manages appointments and waitlists, offers released slots to eligible customers, safely confirms accepted offers and records outcomes.

## Initial scope
- Staff and customer access controls.
- Services, availability and appointment lifecycle.
- Waitlist eligibility and time-limited recovery offers.
- Email delivery tracking.
- Operational dashboard and measured recovery metrics.
- Automated testing, CI and deployment verification.

## Out of scope initially
Payments, native mobile apps, AI recommendations and enterprise multi-branch management.

## Success measures
Recovered completed appointments, eligible cancellation denominator, recovery rate, time to refill, staff effort, notification failures, test results and production reliability. No value is reported as achieved without evidence.
